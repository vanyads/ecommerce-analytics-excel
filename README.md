# ecommerce-analytics-excel
This project analyzes an e-commerce sales dataset from Kaggle containing 5,000 orders from 989 unique customers across four product categories (Beauty, Clothing, Electronics, Home). The data includes order date, region, quantity, unit price, discount, payment method, delivery days, customer rating, and revenue.
# Project Objectives
Identify which product categories drive the most revenue
Compare revenue and order value across low, medium, and high discount levels
Measure how many customers make repeat purchases
Compare customer ratings across product categories
Find which payment methods generate the most revenue
Translate the findings into practical, data-backed business recommendations
# Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Excel — including PivotTables, PivotCharts, and formulas (IF, IFS, COUNTIF).
# Key Findings
- Electronics ($1.83M) and Clothing ($1.53M) drive 65.8% of total revenue
- The 20%+ discount group earns the most revenue ($2.14M) but has the lowest value per order ($920.32)
- 950 of 989 customers are repeat buyers
- The average rating is only 2.97, with little difference across categories
- Card ($2.37M) and COD ($1.79M) lead revenue by payment method
